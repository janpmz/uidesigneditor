About

This is a free design tool to quickly move elements around in your screenshot or HTML design.

You can paste a screenshot or picture into it, select items, move them around and resize them.

This is amazing for some quick prototyping.

Copy the prompt from the editor into your AI terminal and you can tell it to add HTML designs to the editor.

You can use this editor like a canvas together with your AI. For example, the AI makes a design proposal, you edit it, and the AI takes it from there.

When you first visit the website, it creates and saves a "space" for you. This is where all of your projects live.

You can share the space with other people or your AI, and then they can see the projects there.

For example, you could have a project for every page of your website or app.

You can also share the link to a specific project with your AI or other people and ask them to make changes.

This editor is open source. If you find something you want to improve, don't hesitate to fork the repository and make the changes. You can then make a pull request, and I will merge it if I like it.
